RegenCare content platform Subagent roster v1 · 11 Sep 2026

Four agents, one gate

What each autonomous agent does, what it is allowed to touch, and where the human approval sits. Only one of the four can produce anything a patient might read — and only as a draft.

Removed: The Front-Desk & Booking Agent is out of scope following the pivot away from appointment booking. Patient data stays outside the CMS entirely.

The containment chain

Two agents sit on the path that ends at a public page. Two do not touch it at all. The gate between draft and live is always a person.

The agents in detail

01 Competitor Intelligence Highest risk

"Autonomously monitors domestic and international clinic competitors to extract pricing trends, new clinical offerings, and high-performing healthcare content topics."

Runs
Scheduled — nightly or weekly, as a jobs-queue workflow
Reads
Competitor websites — content written by people outside your organisation
Writes
research-reports only — internal, staff-read, never rendered publicly
Scope
Create on research-reports. No access to articles, pages, clinicians or media
Output
A dated report: pricing movements, new service lines, topics competitors rank for

Why it is the highest risk despite writing nothing public: it is the only agent that ingests untrusted external content. A competitor could place hidden instructions on their page. If its output flows onward unlabelled, that is a path from a rival's website into your drafts.

Required control: everything it produces is tagged provenance: untrusted-external, applied by the ingestion layer — never self-reported by the agent. The tag travels downstream.

Still the sensible first build: no publish path, so it needs no approval machinery, and it produces the topic list agent 03 consumes.

02 Product & Discovery Low risk

"Challenges product assumptions, uncovers missing clinical edge cases, and drafts technical specifications."

Runs
On demand, when a team member asks
Reads
Internal documents, the existing content model, the codebase
Writes
specs — internal only
Scope
Create and update on specs. No content access
Output
Specification drafts, edge-case lists, challenges to stated assumptions

Internal inputs, internal outputs, engineers as readers. Nothing it writes is patient-facing. The realistic failure is a wrong spec, caught in review.

03 Marketing & SEO High risk

"Tracks regional healthcare keywords and drafts educational medical blogs for doctors to review."

Runs
Scheduled for keyword tracking; on demand for drafting
Reads
Keyword and search data, existing articles, agent 01's reports
Writes
articles — drafts only
Scope
  • Create on articles, constrained to _status: draft
  • Cannot set _status: published
  • Cannot write medicalReviewer, slug or the canonical URL
Output
Draft articles in the review queue, carrying their provenance tag

The most valuable agent, and the most dangerous. The pivot made content the product, so this moved from nice-to-have to the centre — and it is the only agent with a route, however gated, to a patient's screen.

Two-key approval applies to its output. A marketer checks tone and SEO; a clinician checks the medical claims. Anything carrying untrusted-external provenance needs the clinical signature regardless of topic. The agent that drafts is never an approver.

04 Observability & Sandbox QA Lowest risk

"Simulates synthetic patient flows in a staging environment to detect broken links, latency spikes, or failed booking states."

Scope change from the pivot: "failed booking states" no longer applies. Its remit narrows and re-points at content-platform concerns.

Runs
Scheduled against staging, never production
Reads
The staging site — output you control
Writes
qa-reports
Scope
Read-only on all content; create on qa-reports
Output
  • Broken links, latency regressions, rendering failures
  • Missing metadata or absent JSON-LD
  • Articles published without a reviewer
  • Orphaned pages

It reads a site you control and writes an internal report. It is also the natural place to catch a governance failure — an article that slipped live without credentials.

Build order

Sequential, and the order matters — the gate has to exist before the agent that needs it.

01
Competitor Intelligence

No publish path, so no approval machinery needed. Proves the agent harness works end to end.

02
Observability & QA

Read-only, immediately useful, and it tells you when anything else breaks.

03
Marketing & SEO

Build only after the review queue and dual sign-off exist. This is the one that needs the gate already in place.

04
Product & Discovery

Lowest urgency. Valuable, but not on the critical path.
